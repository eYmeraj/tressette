# Credits

## Card art

The Piacentine card images in `public/cards/` are cut from
[Carte piacentine al completo.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_piacentine_al_completo.jpg)
by Florixc on Wikimedia Commons, released into the **public domain**.
`scripts/slice-cards.cjs` reproduces the crops from the original scan.
